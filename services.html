Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our packages, ongoing support, and how working with Meridian Elevate works.

Is the Retainer mandatory for every client?

Yes. Every business client we work with is placed on an ongoing monthly Retainer. This provides continued support, management and representation after the initial setup phase.

Is the Retainer a one-off payment or a monthly cost?

The Retainer is an ongoing monthly cost. It covers continued management, marketplace support, UK operational assistance and business representation.

Do I need to choose Core Setup or Advantage as well?

Yes, where initial setup services are required. You can choose between Core Setup and Advantage depending on the level of support and infrastructure your business requires. The Retainer then provides your ongoing monthly support.

What is the difference between Core Setup and Advantage?

Core Setup provides the essential foundation for establishing your UK business, including company setup, digital banking, marketplace setup and UK business infrastructure. Advantage includes everything in Core Setup, plus a residential address, UK market development and a strategic business review.

Can I start with Core Setup and upgrade to Advantage later?

Yes. If your business requirements change, you can discuss upgrading your setup and adding the additional services included within Advantage.

What does the monthly Retainer cover?

The Retainer provides ongoing support designed to keep your UK operations and commercial activities running smoothly. It includes UK Hours Management, Limited Company Management, Marketplace Management, and Meeting & Deal Representation.

Are Core Setup and Advantage one-off payments?

Yes. Core Setup and Advantage are project-based setup packages rather than monthly fees. The Retainer is the separate ongoing monthly service.

When does the Retainer start?

The Retainer begins as part of your ongoing client relationship. The start date and billing arrangements will be confirmed with you before the engagement begins.

What happens if I no longer need ongoing support?

If your circumstances change, you can discuss your requirements with us. Any changes to or ending of the Retainer will be handled according to the terms agreed with your business.

Are third-party costs included in the package prices?

The package prices cover the services specifically listed within each package. Government fees, banking charges, marketplace fees, registration costs and other third-party expenses may be separate where applicable. Any additional costs will be discussed with you before they are incurred.

How long does the setup process take?

The timeframe depends on the services required, your documentation, third-party processing times and the complexity of your business. We will provide a clearer expected timeframe during your initial strategy call.

What happens after my company and marketplace setup is complete?

This is where the ongoing Retainer becomes particularly valuable. We continue supporting your UK operations, marketplace activities and commercial requirements rather than simply completing the initial setup and leaving you to manage everything yourself.

Can you represent me in UK meetings and commercial negotiations?

Yes. Meeting & Deal Representation is included within the Retainer. Where appropriate, we can represent your business in relevant UK meetings and commercial discussions.

Do I need to be based in the UK to use the service?

No. Our services are designed to support businesses that want to establish, operate or develop their presence in the UK. Your specific requirements can be discussed during your strategy call.

Can the package be tailored to my business?

Yes. Core Setup and Advantage provide defined service packages, while your ongoing requirements can be discussed with us. If you have requirements outside the listed services, we can determine the most appropriate way to support you.

How does the 15% success fee work?

Where we directly assist in closing a business project or commercial opportunity, a 15% success fee applies to the project value. The success fee becomes payable once the client has paid for that project. This fee is separate from the ongoing monthly Retainer.
