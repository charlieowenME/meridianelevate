Who I Am

I'm Charlie Owen, Founder of Meridian Elevate, with 12+ years of experience in outbound sales and 9+ years working with businesses looking to establish and grow in the UK. My approach is simple: commercial, hands-on and results-focused.

I help international businesses establish, operate and grow their UK presence, from company setup and market analysis through to marketplace management, commercial development and ongoing UK representation.

Before founding Meridian Elevate, I was UK Head of Commercial at ICP Logistica, where I helped transform the business from a small 3PL into a major player across logistics and tech repair. By taking a back-to-basics approach to outbound sales and commercial development, I helped build new opportunities, strengthen client relationships and support the company's growth.

The Journey

Professional Milestones

A journey built through commercial growth, independent consultancy and a commitment to helping international businesses succeed in the UK.

2013 — 2017

Sales, Training & Talent Development

I began my commercial career at DRC Locums as a specialist Radiology Headhunter before progressing into Head of Training & Talent Acquisition. I recruited, trained and developed more than 30 consultants while building a strong foundation in direct sales, commercial development and how different people contribute to a wider revenue operation.

1
2017 — 2021

UK Commercial Growth at ICP Logística

As UK Business Development Director for ICP Logística, I rebuilt much of the UK commercial structure from the ground up including CRM segmentation, enterprise targeting, outbound strategy, commercial messaging, pipeline management and BDR development. During the period, the UK operation expanded from approximately 80,000 to 300,000 sq. ft. and developed from a small-client 3PL portfolio into larger enterprise and multinational accounts.

2
2021 — 2026

Independent Commercial & Learning Development Consultancy

I worked independently across sales operations, commercial performance, training and development, supporting businesses with sales-process improvement, team capability, commercial analysis and business development. This period reinforced my preference for hands-on commercial execution and direct market development rather than advisory-only consultancy.

3
2026 — PRESENT

Founding Meridian Elevate

I founded Meridian Elevate to bring together my experience in UK market development, commercial transformation and sales operations. The focus is helping established international businesses build the UK commercial foundation, representation and market capability required to compete on value rather than geography or price alone.

The Workflow

How I Work

A hands-on approach to taking your business from UK market planning to having an active commercial presence on the ground.

1

Review & Plan

I review your plans, business model and UK ambitions, then we agree on a clear route forward based on what you want to achieve.

2

Build Your UK Base

I set up the foundations for your UK presence, including your Limited Company, UK bank account and dedicated UK workstation and infrastructure.

3

Analyse & Activate

4

Manage & Grow

I manage your UK marketplace presence and ongoing representation while continuing to develop opportunities, refine what works and support sustainable UK growth.
