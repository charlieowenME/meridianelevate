I'm Charlie Owen, Founder at Meridian Elevate

I’m the Founder of Meridian Elevate, helping international businesses establish, grow and compete in the UK market. With 12+ years of outbound sales experience, including 9+ years working with businesses looking to establish and grow in the UK, I provide hands-on commercial support across company setup, market analysis, pitch decks, outbound sales, marketplace management and UK representation. Previously UK Head of Commercial at ICP Logistica, I helped drive its growth from a small 3PL into a major logistics and tech-repair player. I’m commercially driven, straight-talking and results-focused, committed to giving international businesses the opportunity to be valued on their capabilities, not their geography.
