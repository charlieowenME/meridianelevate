Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our packages, ongoing support, and how working with Meridian Elevate works.

What does Meridian Elevate help international businesses with?

Meridian Elevate helps international businesses establish and develop a working UK commercial presence. Depending on the package, this can include UK company formation, registered business infrastructure, banking and payment-provider application support, UK communications, marketplace setup with an included launch allowance, an initial UK prospect databank, managed email outreach, website review and positioning, ongoing UK representation and commercial development.

Do I need to be based in the UK to use the service?

No. The service is designed specifically to support international businesses that want to establish, operate or develop their presence in the UK.

Do I need to travel to the UK to set up my business?

Not necessarily. Much of the initial setup can be handled remotely. The exact requirements depend on the company, banking provider and services required.

Can you help me set up a UK limited company?

Yes. We assist international clients with the practical process of establishing a UK limited company, including coordinating the required information and formation process.

Can you help with setting up a UK business bank account?

Yes. We assist with the practical application process for a suitable digital business banking or payment solution and help coordinate the information and documentation requested by the provider. Depending on eligibility and requirements, suitable providers may include Airwallex, WorldFirst, Revolut Business or another appropriate provider. Account approval and availability always remain with the provider.

Can an international director own and operate a UK company?

Yes. Our service is designed to support international directors and business owners establishing and operating a UK company. We assist with the practical setup and ongoing UK requirements.

What is the difference between Essential, Advantage and Premium?

Essential establishes the UK commercial foundation with one actively managed marketplace, an initial UK prospect databank, one managed email campaign per month and UK website guidance. Advantage increases this to two marketplaces, two managed campaigns per month, deeper website recommendations and closer integration with your sales and development teams. Premium provides the deepest involvement with three marketplaces, four managed campaigns per month, UK copywriting, SEO and blog support, and Meridian working alongside your existing sales operation on UK opportunity development, market intelligence and commercial improvement.

What is included in the Essential package?

Essential provides the core UK operating foundation, including company setup support, UK business infrastructure and banking preparation where applicable. It also includes one actively managed marketplace, a preloaded marketplace launch allowance, an initial UK prospect databank of up to 1,000 companies, one managed email campaign per month and a UK website review with practical UK-market guidance. Qualified responses and opportunities are then moved into the agreed sales process.

What additional support does Advantage provide?

Advantage includes the Essential foundation but increases the level of active UK support. Meridian manages two agreed marketplaces with a larger launch allowance, an initial prospect databank of up to 1,500 companies and two managed email campaigns per month. Meridian also works more closely with your sales and development teams, provides deeper UK website recommendations, supports UK-facing correspondence and can handle agreed inbound calls and callbacks. A suitable client-domain email account is provided for agreed outreach and representation so communication remains consistent with your business.

What makes Premium different from Advantage?

Premium is designed for businesses that want Meridian deeply embedded in UK commercial development. It includes three actively managed marketplaces with the largest launch allowance, an initial prospect databank of up to 2,500 companies and four managed email campaigns per month. Meridian can also create UK-focused website copy, SEO content and blog material while working alongside your existing sales operation to develop opportunities, analyse progress and improve qualification, messaging, follow-up, CRM discipline and conversion.

Do the packages include ongoing monthly support?

Yes. The setup fee establishes and funds the initial launch infrastructure, including the package's initial marketplace allowance, prospect databank and first operating period. The monthly retainer then maintains the ongoing UK operation, including the applicable marketplace activity, managed outreach, communications and other package services. The retainer begins once the initial setup is complete and the ongoing service is ready to commence.

Are third-party costs included in the package prices?

The package prices include the allowances and services specifically shown within each package, including the stated marketplace launch allowance and initial prospect databank. Exceptional third-party costs or additional platform, data, advertising, banking, professional-provider or other spend beyond the included allowances will always be discussed and agreed before it is incurred.

How long does the setup process take?

The timeframe depends on the services required, the information provided, third-party processing times and the complexity of the business. We will provide a clearer expected timeframe during the initial process. The ongoing monthly retainer only begins once the initial setup has been completed and the ongoing service is ready to commence.

What happens after my UK company and initial setup are complete?

The company setup is only one part of the process. Meridian first establishes the operating foundation, marketplace access, communications, initial prospect data, CRM routes and commercial authority parameters while learning your services, customers, pricing, capacity and sales process. Marketplace activity can begin during the launch period, while direct email outreach is deliberately prepared around that learning so the first major outreach push is based on a stronger understanding of the UK proposition.

Can you represent my business in the UK?

Yes. The level of representation depends on the package selected. Essential combines marketplace opportunity development, managed outreach and handover. Advantage increases UK correspondence, telephone communication, outreach frequency and sales-team integration. Premium provides the deepest integration, with Meridian working directly alongside your existing commercial team and supporting wider UK positioning, content and opportunity development.

Do you research the UK market and competitors before going live?

Yes. Every engagement requires an understanding of where your existing strengths fit within the UK market before commercial activity begins. The depth of research increases with the package, with higher levels allowing for more detailed competitor analysis, account segmentation, stakeholder mapping, opportunity signals and ongoing market intelligence.

How do you decide which UK opportunities to pursue?

Before go-live, we agree clear commercial parameters covering the services you want to sell, preferred project values, minimum pricing, project duration, delivery capacity, payment expectations and situations that require your approval. This allows Meridian to quickly identify which opportunities can be pursued directly and which should be referred to your sales or technical team first.

Will Meridian replace my existing sales team?

No. Meridian is designed to connect into your existing commercial operation rather than build a separate sales team beside it. I work with a direct contact inside your sales function, bring your people into opportunities when required and, particularly at Premium level, use live UK activity to help improve the way your existing sales operation performs.

What happens when Meridian develops a genuine sales opportunity?

The opportunity is recorded in the agreed CRM and your sales contact is brought into the process at the appropriate stage. Meridian can continue supporting the UK-facing conversation according to your package, while your team provides technical, pricing and delivery input where required. Once the customer commits, the relationship is formally introduced to your Account Manager and technical or delivery team.

Can Meridian Elevate help develop actual UK business opportunities?

Yes. Opportunity development begins at Essential through both an actively managed marketplace and direct UK outreach, then becomes progressively more involved through Advantage and Premium. Depending on the package, Meridian can identify marketplace opportunities, submit bids, create and run approved HTML email campaigns, monitor responses, support UK communication, work alongside your sales team and help develop suitable opportunities through the commercial process.

Do you create the email campaigns for us?

Yes. Meridian can create the campaign structure, messaging and HTML email design for the agreed UK outreach activity. The campaign is prepared around your offer and target audience and can be sent to you for approval before it goes live, so you do not need to arrive with finished campaign artwork or HTML.

How many managed email campaigns are included?

The current package structure provides one managed campaign per month with Essential, two per month with Advantage and four per month with Premium. Campaign activity is coordinated with the agreed UK prospect data, positioning and commercial objectives rather than being treated as bulk sending for its own sake.

What email address will the campaigns be sent from?

Where required for the agreed activity, the client provides Meridian with an appropriate email account on the client's own business domain. This keeps UK-facing communication consistent with the company being represented. The exact sending configuration is agreed during setup.

Do I need to provide my own UK prospect list?

No. The launch setup includes an initial UK prospect databank according to the package selected. Meridian develops the targeting brief from your services, customers, preferred sectors, commercial parameters and initial UK research, then suitable B2B data can be sourced for the campaign programme.

What happens when the initial prospect data has been used?

The data strategy is designed to improve over time. Around the six-month commercial review point, Meridian can assess response quality, sectors, buyer roles, objections, opportunities and the quality of the original records. A new or replenished data brief can then be built around what the UK market has actually taught us, creating a more tailored next batch.

Why not buy the most targeted prospect data immediately?

The initial specification is based on the best evidence available at launch, but a new UK operation has not yet generated its own market evidence. After several months of real outreach and opportunity development, Meridian can give the data provider a much stronger brief based on actual responses, useful contacts, weak segments and commercial results.

Are marketplace credits included when we launch?

Yes. Each package includes a defined marketplace launch allowance so the accounts can begin operating without waiting for individual opportunity costs to be approved one by one. The allowance varies by package and is used for appropriate marketplace activity within the agreed commercial parameters.

What happens to marketplace costs after the launch period?

The ongoing service includes a package-level marketplace operating allowance for continued activity. Meridian manages that allowance against suitable opportunities rather than spending simply to exhaust a budget. Any exceptional platform spend beyond the included allowance is discussed and agreed in advance.

Do email campaigns start immediately after the company is formed?

Not necessarily. The first stage is used to establish the UK operation and properly understand your offer, customers, pricing, website, sales process and target market. Marketplace activity can help generate early learning, while the main direct-outreach push is prepared around that knowledge. The operating plan is designed to move from setup and learning into a stronger outreach phase rather than sending generic campaigns on day one.

Can you improve the campaign after seeing how UK companies respond?

Yes. Response monitoring and refinement are part of the managed outreach process, with the depth increasing by package. Meridian can use live UK feedback to improve targeting, messaging and future campaign direction. That learning also feeds into the six-month data review so the next prospect batch and commercial push can be more precisely tailored.
