What Actually Happens After You Choose Meridian Elevate?

So you've seen the package that fits your business, we've sent you the proposal and quotation, you've accepted it and paid the setup fee. What happens next?

The first job is to build your UK operating foundation, collect the information required from your business, prepare the commercial channels and establish how opportunities will be managed once they begin to arrive.

Implementation takes approximately 4–8 weeks. During this period we establish the agreed UK infrastructure, complete onboarding, connect the operation into your existing business and prepare the commercial channels for launch. Company formation, verification, banking, marketplace approvals and other third-party processes can affect the final timeline.

Your monthly retainer does not begin during implementation. Your first retainer becomes payable on your UK Launch Date, once the agreed operation is ready to go live.

Step 1: Your Onboarding Starts After Signing & Payment

Once your proposal is signed and the setup fee has been received, I issue the Meridian Elevate onboarding documents for your selected package. These capture the company information, commercial contacts, responsibilities, approvals, access and operational requirements needed to begin building your UK operation.

Your onboarding documents will include clear return dates for the information required from your business. These dates form part of the implementation schedule because company formation, systems, commercial preparation and launch readiness all depend on the right information being available at the right stage.

From this point, implementation becomes a managed delivery process. I establish the infrastructure included within your package while we progressively connect your existing business, commercial capability and sales operation into the new UK foundation.

Step 2: I Build Your UK Operating Foundation

With the required company information in place, I begin establishing the UK operating foundation included within your package. Where a standard UK Limited Company structure is appropriate, I coordinate the incorporation process using the information and authority you provide. Where specialist ownership, legal, accounting or tax advice is required, the relevant element is referred to an appropriate professional adviser.

Company formation is only one part of the implementation. The commercial objective is to establish a practical UK operating environment around the company giving your business the local infrastructure, communications, systems and access required to support commercial activity and future growth.

“A UK company creates the legal presence. The operating foundation behind it is what gives that presence the structure to support commercial growth.”

Step 3: We Connect the UK Operation to Your Business

The UK operation needs to function as an extension of your existing business rather than as a disconnected sales channel. We therefore connect the new UK structure into the systems, people and commercial processes you already use.

You provide an appropriate email account on your existing company domain for the agreed Meridian representative activity. Where you already operate a suitable cloud CRM, I work through my own authorised user account so prospect activity, opportunities and handovers remain visible within your commercial environment.

WhatsApp provides the fast commercial route for time-sensitive approvals, quotations and marketplace opportunities. The CRM remains the structured record of commercial activity, while WhatsApp allows us to make quick decisions when speed matters.

Step 4: We Translate Your Existing Commercial Model into the UK Market

The objective is not to reinvent a business model that already works. We start by understanding what you currently sell, how you sell it, who already buys from you and where your strongest commercial results are being generated.

We then translate that existing offer into the UK market. That means assessing where your products or services fit within UK and wider Western industry structures, how buyers typically organise procurement and supplier relationships, which sectors offer the strongest alignment and where your proposition may need to be repositioned for local expectations.

The result is a commercial starting point built around proven capability rather than assumptions — preserving what already works while adapting how it is positioned, targeted and presented to UK buyers.

Step 5: I Map the UK Market and Build Your Initial Prospect Base

Once we understand where your existing offer translates into the UK market, I turn that commercial direction into a structured targeting specification. The objective is not to produce a large generic database; it is to identify companies that match the sectors, buyer profiles, operating characteristics and commercial situations we have agreed are worth pursuing.

Your initial prospect databank contains up to 1,000 companies for Essential , 1,500 for Advantage and 2,500 for Premium .

Advantage introduces deeper segmentation around the agreed commercial plan. Premium extends this further through competitor and incumbent analysis looking at how target companies are currently served, what those supplier relationships may reveal and where commercial gaps, weaknesses or alternative buying situations could create an opening for your proposition.

Step 7: I Prepare the Commercial Channels for Launch

During implementation I establish, prepare and optimise the agreed marketplace profiles, accounts and service offerings. In parallel, I develop the initial outbound strategy, messaging, campaign design and HTML required to begin direct market activity.

Key campaign material is presented to you for approval before it enters the market. We review any requested changes against the commercial objective of the campaign, refine where necessary and agree the final material that represents your business.

Implementation prepares the channels. UK Launch Date activates them. Marketplace activity and outbound campaigns remain prepared but inactive until the operation has passed its Launch Readiness Gate.

Step 8: We Establish Commercial Authority Before Opportunities Arrive

Before launch, we establish a clear commercial authority matrix so opportunities can move at the pace expected by UK buyers. We agree the parameters I can operate within, including relevant pricing, discount boundaries, project values, contract length, capacity, delivery requirements and payment terms.

Where an opportunity sits within those agreed parameters, I can keep the commercial conversation moving. Where a decision falls outside them, it is escalated quickly through the agreed route including WhatsApp where immediate approval is commercially valuable. You retain control while removing unnecessary delay from the sales process.

What Happens When a Genuine UK Opportunity Appears?

Every package connects market activity into a defined commercial process. What changes is the point at which the opportunity moves into your team and how deeply I remain involved in developing it.

Essential

Response → assess and qualify → CRM / agreed handover → your sales team leads the substantive sales process → outcome and market learning recorded.

Advantage

Response → qualify → develop the opportunity → introduce your salesperson → coordinate the UK-facing conversation → outcome and commercial intelligence feed into future activity.

Premium

Response → qualification and discovery → CRM → work alongside your sales team → proposal and progression → follow-up → outcome → buyer and competitor intelligence feeds back into targeting, messaging and sales development.
